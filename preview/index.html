What it shows

  • Claude — your 5-hour session and weekly usage, with reset countdowns.
  • ChatGPT — message limits for your plan and how much of the window is gone.
  • Toolbar badge — the percentage left on your tightest limit.
  • Warnings — the card turns amber under 15% left and red at the limit.

Install it

  1. 1
    Keep the extension/ folder from this repository on your machine.
  2. 2
    Open your browser's extensions page — chrome://extensions, edge://extensions or opera://extensions — and turn on Developer mode.
  3. 3
    Choose Load unpacked, select the extension/ folder, then pin the icon to the toolbar.

Works in Chrome, Edge, Opera, Brave, Vivaldi and Arc — any Chromium browser with Manifest V3 support.

How it reads usage

A small content script runs on claude.ai and chatgpt.com while you're signed in. It asks the site for the same usage numbers its own settings screen shows, and falls back to the meters and limit banners already on the page. Everything is cached in the browser's local extension storage.

  • No account, no API keys, no server — nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Reads only the two sites you are already signed in to.
  • Refreshes when you open the popup, when a tab becomes visible, and every five minutes.
